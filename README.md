### What I do

At **Renegade Insurance** I architected **AgencyHeight**, the Next.js marketplace used by 3,000+ insurance agents. It's a Turborepo monorepo with 10+ shared packages, and it shipped with a 30% smaller bundle and 46% better Core Web Vitals than the platform it replaced.

Before that, at Young Innovations, I built the visualization layer of Nepal's national census portal.

I'm open to remote frontend roles.
